Research Overview & Motivation

Why This Research Matters

Information Retrieval Challenge: Modern IR systems require precise document ranking from vast collections

Cross-Encoder Advantage: State-of-the-art reranking through deep query-document interactions

Optimization Gap: Limited research on Lion optimizer for IR tasks despite success in other domains

Practical Impact: Optimizer choice significantly affects training cost and model performance

Research Problem:
How do Lion and AdamW optimizers compare across different cross-encoder architectures for information retrieval tasks?

Research Objectives

Primary Objectives

  1. Systematic Evaluation: Compare Lion and AdamW across three transformer architectures
  2. Comprehensive Benchmarking: Evaluate performance using standard IR metrics on TREC DL 2019 and MS MARCO
  3. Training Dynamics Analysis: Investigate convergence patterns and stability
  4. Practical Guidelines: Provide evidence-based recommendations for optimizer selection

Expected Contributions

• Empirical evidence comparing optimizers across multiple architectures

• Optimization guidelines for cross-encoder training in IR

• Analysis of training efficiency and resource utilization

Optimizer Comparison

AdamW vs Lion: Key Differences

Aspect AdamW Lion
Update Rule Adaptive learning rates with momentum Sign-based momentum
Memory Usage Higher (stores gradients + variance) Lower (momentum only)
Computation Complex adaptive scaling Simple sign operation
Tuning Well-established hyperparameters Requires careful tuning
Novelty Standard for transformers Recent innovation
Lion's Innovation: update = sign(momentum) × learning_rate

Experimental Setup

Dataset Configuration

Training: MS MARCO Passage Ranking (~500K query-passage pairs)

Evaluation: TREC DL 2019 (43 queries with comprehensive relevance judgments)

Metrics: NDCG@10, MAP, MRR@10, Recall@10, R-Precision, P@10

Infrastructure

Platform: Modal cloud computing

Hardware: 3 × NVIDIA L40S GPUs

Monitoring: Weights & Biases

Training: 3 epochs per configuration

Experimental Design

Total Configurations: 6 (3 models × 2 optimizers)

Controlled Variables: Batch size, training steps, evaluation frequency

Variable Parameters: Learning rates (2e-5 standard, 2e-6 for ModernBERT+Lion)
