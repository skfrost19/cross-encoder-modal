\chapter{Summary \& Future Scope of Work}
\label{Chapter6}

This chapter summarizes the key contributions of our research and outlines promising directions for future investigation in optimizer effectiveness for cross-encoder reranking.

\section{Research Summary}

This thesis investigated the comparative effectiveness of Lion and AdamW optimizers for cross-encoder reranking across three transformer architectures: MiniLM, GTE, and ModernBERT.

\subsection{Primary Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{First Systematic Evaluation}: Conducted the first comprehensive comparison of Lion optimizer for cross-encoder information retrieval tasks across multiple architectures.
    
    \item \textbf{Architecture-Optimizer Interaction Analysis}: Demonstrated that optimizer effectiveness varies significantly based on model architecture and training configuration.
    
    \item \textbf{State-of-the-art Results}: Achieved best-in-class performance with ModernBERT + Lion (NDCG@10: 0.7225) and tied best MRR@10 (0.5988) on standard IR benchmarks.
    
    \item \textbf{Training Dynamics Insights}: Provided detailed analysis of convergence patterns and gradient behavior differences between optimizers.
    
    \item \textbf{Resource Efficiency Analysis}: Conducted comprehensive GPU usage monitoring revealing Lion's consistent efficiency advantages across all tested models.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Findings Summary}

Our experimental results establish that:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Universal Resource Efficiency}: Lion optimizer achieved lower GPU utilization across 100\% of tested configurations, with efficiency gains ranging from 2.67\% to 10.33\%
    \item \textbf{Performance-Efficiency Trade-offs}: While neither optimizer consistently outperforms across all ranking metrics, Lion provides compelling advantages when considering both performance and computational cost
    \item \textbf{Configuration Sensitivity}: Optimizer effectiveness depends heavily on learning rate and scheduling choices, with Lion requiring careful tuning for optimal results
    \item \textbf{Modern Architecture Benefits}: Advanced transformer features synergize well with Lion's optimization approach, showing both performance and efficiency improvements
    \item \textbf{Scalable Efficiency Gains}: Larger models demonstrated more substantial efficiency improvements with Lion, making it particularly attractive for large-scale deployments
\end{itemize}

\section{Practical Recommendations}

For practitioners working with cross-encoder reranking:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Consider Resource Efficiency}: Evaluate Lion optimizer for its proven 5.50\% average efficiency improvement across all model architectures, particularly valuable in production environments
    \item \textbf{Model-Specific Optimization}: 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Use Lion with ModernBERT for optimal performance and efficiency combination
        \item Consider Lion for resource-constrained GTE deployments despite slight ranking performance trade-offs
        \item Evaluate Lion for MiniLM in MS MARCO-style tasks
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Monitor Training Dynamics}: Track both performance metrics and resource utilization for comprehensive optimization assessment
    \item \textbf{Architecture-Specific Tuning}: Adjust optimizer parameters based on model architecture characteristics, with Lion showing scalable benefits for larger models
    \item \textbf{Cost-Benefit Analysis}: Factor in the 2.67\% to 10.33\% efficiency gains when selecting optimizers for large-scale or long-running training jobs
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

This thesis demonstrates that optimizer choice significantly impacts both cross-encoder reranking performance and computational efficiency, with effectiveness varying by architecture and configuration. Our comprehensive analysis reveals that Lion optimizer provides universal resource efficiency benefits (5.50\% average improvement) across all tested models while maintaining competitive or superior performance in many scenarios. The Lion optimizer shows particular promise for modern transformer architectures when properly tuned, offering both performance gains and substantial cost savings in production environments. These findings provide both practical guidance for practitioners seeking to optimize their inference systems and a foundation for future research in efficient neural information retrieval optimization.
